# Warm oak — vertical native game material

Create one square, seamless, edge-to-edge pixel-art wood SURFACE TEXTURE for an original cozy-tech office game. This is flat material only, not a rendered desk, plank object, room, furniture, floor scene or perspective view. Logical tile32×32 pixels enlarged cleanly; every cluster snaps to that coarse native grid. Wood grain runs vertically top-to-bottom in sparse short darker streaks, with small warm highlights. This is independently authored vertical grain, not a rotated furniture picture. No thick plank seams, nails, knots shaped like eyes, directional cast shadow, bevel, outer border or margin. Opposite edges tile seamlessly both ways. Simple calm honey-oak, low visual noise so tiny characters and tools remain readable above it.

Restrained material colors: dark#754326,#8F4B24; middle#A75927,#B7672C,#C47734; highlight#D58A42,#E3A45A. Use flat clustered colors without photographic grain, smooth gradients or antialiasing. Avoid bright neon orange or chocolate-black surfaces. Keep the whole texture evenly illuminated; object edge lighting will be separate game geometry. Output square full-bleed opaque RGB, not transparency or a checkerboard. No text, logos, icons, labels or objects.
