# Legal planning toolkit — north-use original art

Use case: stylized-concept. Asset type: modular pixel-art tabletop toolkit for Startup Valley, not a whole desk or room.

Create exactly three separate reusable desk objects for a startup's lawyer: a short stack of two charcoal-and-indigo contract reference binders with cream page edges; an open indigo review folder showing an off-white checklist with three broad dark marks and two chartreuse index tabs; a small charcoal pen cup with two pens and one warm brass pencil. This should feel like useful contract review and business planning, not courtroom cosplay. No gavel, scales of justice, wig, flag, badge, logo or actual legal wording.

Fixed camera: square, screen-aligned orthographic overhead/front-elevation cozy game. The user of this north-facing workstation sits SOUTH and looks NORTH. Binder spine and folder readable edge face the bottom of the screen. Paper/folder long axis runs vertical, checklist header at the top. Keep all top-plane edges parallel to screen X/Y, no diamond-isometric yaw, diagonal rectangles, trapezoidal perspective or vanishing point. Binder stack shows a modest visible top and short front page/spine elevation; pen cup is vertical with a small visible top opening. Light always from upper-left.

Target native proportions for independently exported objects: binder stack20px wide×12px floor depth, about6px stack rise; open folder18px wide×22px floor depth, about1px thickness; pen cup6×6px floor footprint and12px total height including pens. Each fits a side area of a96×32px desk top beside—not instead of—the central computer. These are proportion targets, not labels to draw. Render an enlarged hard-pixel master with crisp clustered colors and minimal detail that will survive native size.

Palette: warm cream/off-white#F1F1F1, charcoal#151515, indigo#354B98, muted gray#B6B5B0, restrained chartreuse#C1FA81 tabs; one tiny vermilion#FA4616 binder clip is allowed. Avoid heavy gradients, neon, fine unreadable typography or noisy texture. Original Startup Valley art only.

Arrange the three COMPLETE objects with generous transparent gutters and padding, all visible separately, not overlapping. True RGBA transparency outside every object; no checkerboard image, matte background, floor, cast shadow, desk, chair, employee, monitor, frame, border, label or extra objects. This is ONE north-use toolkit view. Other directions will be authored separately.
