## Native result

The shelf sits at a plausible low, desk-height scale beside the actual founder,
Mina and Theo. Its South opening reads as books plus a pale storage bin, without
text or tiny invented controls. The material is warmer and simpler than the
nearby detailed production desks. Its dark opening is clean at1×; the extra9px
gutters make the content look deliberately inset but also slightly sparse.
The source contains several nearly-black background colors, so this is not a
claim of a perfectly flat continuous generated background.

North correctly shows an empty wooden rear rather than books. East and West
show closed ends and a long top; they can be mistaken for a narrow cabinet or
bench, but showing South's books on those hidden faces would be false. The
camera, floor-axis projection and actual32px rise are unchanged. The simple
recessed panels and solid plinth are reusable construction, not finished ornate
furniture. No new generation is recommended merely to conceal the edge view.
