## Direction and placement contract

**Workstation facing means employee gaze toward the work surface**, not the desk-front camera view. A north-facing desk is used by a worker on its south side looking north. Host role kits inherit this gaze convention. Shared-service objects instead explicitly declare **`service_side`** and the opposite **`approach_gaze`**; a south-front archive is used from the south while the actor looks north. The old single convention incorrectly treated these as the same direction.

The direction-semantics checkpoint fixes **14 service families /56 planning variants**: `coffee_station`, `artifact_archive`, `approval_console`, `mobile_whiteboard`, `rolling_storage_pedestal`, `low_bookshelf`, `tall_storage_cabinet`, `water_station`, `coat_bag_stand`, `printer_scanner_station`, `recycling_sorter`, `server_cabinet`, `phone_booth`, `resource_cart`. Service N/E/S/W uses approach gaze S/W/N/E. Masks and interaction cells are reindexed from the opposite old gaze slot, not merely given a flipped label: this prevents the user being placed inside deeper masks. For the three existing service families—coffee, archive, approval—the new South planning mask/interaction exactly preserves the existing runtime South geometry. That earlier planning checkpoint changed no production layout or save; later family-specific reviews govern actual promotions.

The other28 families retain their prior geometry/gaze data and now state what those labels mean: five workstations (`desk`, `research_table`, `prototype_bench`, `compact_desk`, `shared_workbench`); four occupant-facing seats; three multi-seat reference layouts; four visual-orientation-only props (`low_coffee_table`, `acoustic_divider`, `broadleaf_planter`, `task_floor_lamp`); twelve host-attached kits. See the [coffee/archive audit](COFFEE_ARCHIVE_DIRECTION_BRIEF.md) for the proven legacy naming contradiction and native evidence.

Coordinates are Godot 2D: east/right is positive x; south/down is positive y. The four look vectors are `N=[0,-1]`, `E=[1,0]`, `S=[0,1]`, `W=[-1,0]`.

Each family has one proposed or explicitly measured dev-native canvas and one fixed `floor_anchor_px`. That exact pixel represents local floor pivot `[0,0]` in **every direction and every layer**. Different-family canvases may differ; never crop or recenter one facing independently. Padding accommodates taller/back/side views and is not permission to enlarge the visible furniture or downscale it in game.

For base furniture, `proposed_occupied_cell_offsets` supplies an explicit rectangular planning mask for each direction. Quarter-turns of the **logical mask** make a 3×2 footprint become 2×3 east/west while retaining the floor pivot. Rotating a whole sprite to make the matching art is prohibited: redraw the projected back, side, monitor, drawers, shadows and occlusion correctly.

These are proposed occupancy masks, not validated collision data. The three legacy service-family South masks are preserved exactly; other proposal-to-runtime differences still need a deliberate migration. The proposed interaction cell identifies the approach side; it does not claim a tested route or seat socket. Multi-seat tables/sofas need named seats, approach clearance and separate actor sockets before runtime use. ## Bases, kits and layers

Bases provide structure and a physical footprint, with a separate floor shadow when explicitly authored; solid feet, rails or plinths are not shadow substitutes. A role kit provides detachable surface tools—not another desk, chair, employee, wall or floor. This lets a legal-planning desk and a creative desk share furniture without looking identical.

Use the same canvas/anchor for all layers of an export:

- `floor_shadow`: ground only.
- `rear_structure` / `front_structure`: actual far/near structure around actor silhouettes.
- `seat_back` / `seat_front`: chair-specific occlusion, with the worker supplied separately.
- `surface_decor_rear` / `surface_decor_front`: detachable tools around hands and torso.
- `ambient_overlay`: optional independent steam, light or screen motion, never baked permanent “working” status.

A desk and its standalone task chair are separate families; do not bake a second chair into the new desk art. Current legacy layer metadata remains evidence, not the new assembly recipe. Layer names are semantic: verify the actual draw order for each direction rather than copying south-facing z offsets.

Kits declare one `primary_host`, share its native canvas and anchor, and add zero floor collision. A second host requires an explicitly reviewed socket adaptation; it is not automatically compatible just because it is another desk. The catalogue's 168 count covers only the primary directional export set. Additional host adaptations and animations must be tracked separately.

Future runtime integration is a separate adapter into reviewed per-direction `facing_profiles` (occupied/clearance/interaction/work-cell geometry, anchor/visual bounds, art and layer data). Workstations additionally need validated `work_facing`, `seat_anchor`, `seat_z_offset` and an actual compatible actor `pose_set`. Do not enable a new runtime facing just because this planning catalogue has an entry for it. Preserve legacy south behavior until real art, geometry and actor poses pass review.

The runtime profile's `status: "runtime_ready"` and `review: {art, geometry, poses}` flags are a **different schema** from this catalogue's production lifecycle. A reviewed integration step maps them explicitly and supplies actual `render_layers`/`ambient_overlays`; do not copy a catalogue status or review object into runtime data.

Founder/Mina/product/Theo/growth/lawyer/creative/engineer/finance/customer-success kits describe **art identity and visible tools only**. 5. Separate collision, approach and visual-wall-clearance checks; no clipping through walls or circulation lanes.
6. Real evidence paths with cleanup and review disposition recorded.

| `rejected` | Existing candidate output plus a concise `reason`; never remove its failed status to make the gallery look complete. |
| `blocked` | A `reason`; either missing with no output or a truthful existing candidate. |
| `native_review_required` | At least one declared native export and its real PNG; approval remains null. |
| `approved` | Real `approved_art_path`, native `evidence_path`, every required layer exported, and explicit passing review fields below. |
| `runtime_integrated` | All approval prerequisites plus actual `runtime_art_path` or `runtime_exports`, and `runtime_evidence_path`. It is not inferred from a file living in a runtime folder. |

`native_exports` and `runtime_exports` are arrays of descriptors with `layer`, `path`, `canvas_size_px` and `floor_anchor_px`. The declared geometry must match the family; actual PNG dimensions must match the canvas. An atlas descriptor additionally uses `atlas_grid: [columns, rows]` and `layer_ids`; each cell retains the family canvas and shared anchor. Do not pretend a source master or legacy layer atlas has the new native envelope. A single non-atlas `native_export_path` is supported for review candidates, but approval still requires the complete named layer descriptors.

An approved variant's `review` records `geometry_reviewed: true`, `placement_tests_passed: true`, and `actor_pose_reviewed: true` when a seated pose is needed. Its `scores` use exactly these keys: `pixel_coherence`, `perspective`, `silhouette_readability`, `layering_anchors`, `direction_consistency`, `placement`. Every score must be an integer from 0–3; approval requires every score at least 2 and a mean of at least 2.5. These are review claims backed by existing evidence, not visual conclusions the validator can independently establish.

Use `prompt_path` and source/output/evidence path fields for real provenance. Paths are repository-root-relative or `res://` game paths. Do not insert guessed filenames, external temporary paths or an approved path for an unreviewed view. `summary.approved_directional_deliverables` counts both approved and runtime-integrated slots; the historical `planned_directional_deliverables` field remains the total required slots, not just those still waiting.

The self-test mode exercises valid lifecycle transitions and rejects false approval, absent files, dimension/anchor/mask drift, bad kits and failed review criteria. Its accepted-state virtual files exist **only in memory**; it never adds example approvals or fixture art to the catalogue/gallery. Passing tests do not certify the pictures.
