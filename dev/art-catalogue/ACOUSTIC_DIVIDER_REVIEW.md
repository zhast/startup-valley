2026-09-04 10:51 UTC. **Bounded staged prototype; no runtime admission.** Geometry,
canonical placement and observed actor depth pass. The parent independently

## Native result and recommendation

The broad fabric face is quiet at1×. No obvious repeating stripe, rectangular
tile seam or bright texture distraction appears across its84×52 interior. The
existing6-color source remains unchanged. Its charcoal rim fits the office's
dark furniture details, but the large flat face and small feet also resemble a
freestanding screen. This is not yet a distinctive finished furniture identity.

The six-pixel E/W edge is geometrically honest but reads as a pole with cross
feet at native size. The long highlighted strip can be interpreted as a tall
post, despite its actual88px floor depth plus56px vertical face. This is the
main reason not to promote this candidate merely because rotation works.
The straight frame finish and simple foot caps are secondary polish limits.

The physical height is plausible beside the actual48×80 actors. A walking
actor behind the broad panel keeps their head visible while their body is
occluded; the same actor in front draws above it. No new pose, shadow or fake
writing/interaction is implied.

## Exact construction and provenance

The [agreed contract](ACOUSTIC_DIVIDER_CONTRACT.md) is implemented in
[acoustic_divider_geometry.gd](../../game/tools/acoustic_divider_geometry.gd).
The sole generated original used here is the existing parent-produced felt
material documented in [ACOUSTIC_FELT_REVIEW.md](ACOUSTIC_FELT_REVIEW.md).
This task generated no new images. The frame and flat feet are code-native
charcoal construction, not reused caster/chair imagery.

- Every view/layer uses a192×224 canvas, pivot(96,160), native centered offset
  (0,−48), fixed upper-left light, no runtime scaling/mirroring/rotation.
- Broad N/S face is(-44,−61,88,56); its6px top plane is(-44,−67,88,6).
  The2px frame encloses unmodified felt at(-42,−59,84,52).
- Narrow E/W top plane is(-3,−108,6,88); its near end begins at y−20 and extends
 56px to y36. The seam at−20 separates physical top depth from vertical end.
- Occupied footprint is3×1 N/S,1×3 E/W. Conservative floor bounds are96×32
  and32×96. Two flat T feet stay within those bounds; there is no caster claim.
- Exact native opaque bounds are **(52,93,88,83) N/S**, **(80,52,32,148) E/W**.
  The conservative visual bounds are a little larger where the floor envelope
  exceeds actual foot ink. Collision is not inferred from transparent padding.
- Layers in draw order: RearSupport, Panel, FrontFoot, all at the same host
  floor depth. Each part retains full registered padding.
- N and S are byte-identical; E and W are byte-identical. That is deliberate
  physical equivalence for a double-sided symmetric construction, **not four
  independently generated assets**. There are two distinct projected shapes.
- [Versioned exports](../../game/tests/fixtures/furniture-calibration/acoustic-divider-v1/)
  contain12 registered parts +4 whole PNGs. All16 rebuild byte-identically;
  import alpha-border fixing is disabled and decoded RGBA remains exact.

lossless/unchanged-alpha settings, then run the test:

~~~sh
godot --headless --path game --script res://tests/test_acoustic_divider.gd -- --export
godot --headless --path game --import
godot --headless --path game --fixed-fps 30 --script res://tests/test_acoustic_divider.gd
godot --path game --fixed-fps 30 --write-movie res://tests/output/acoustic-divider/world.avi --script res://tests/test_acoustic_divider.gd
~~~
