# A small beginning in a warm village

The player starts a tiny venture in a quiet, modest neighborhood. It should
feel cute, hopeful and lived-in, not corporate, luxurious or shabby for comic
effect. Stardew Valley's everyday warmth and the peaceful residential opening
of Pokémon are mood references; our characters, assets and place are original.

## Starter vocabulary

- A small rented room or adapted village workshop, with plain wood, cream
  fabric, modest windows, a few useful shelves and signs of people using it.
- Simple wooden chairs with small cushions, rounded readable silhouettes,
  restrained warm contours and matching pixel detail. Avoid premium ergonomic
  mesh chairs, executive furniture, chrome office suites and ornate upholstery.
- A hand-me-down computer, mug, desk lamp, pinboard and one small plant can
  express personality. Make a few objects charming instead of filling every
  space with expensive equipment or decorative clutter.
- A quiet shared yard or residential lane: grass, a short path, simple fencing,
  a mailbox/noticeboard and a few garden plants. The early exterior should not
  imply ownership of an estate, resort, waterfall garden or grand pavilion.
- Keep the established camera, 32px grid, actor size, warm material palette,
  readable movement routes and shared furniture landmarks. Cute does not mean
  oversized furniture or inconsistent proportions.

## Progress should change the place

| Stage | Environmental promise |
| --- | --- |
| New company | One modest working space and essential everyday objects. No free complete catalogue of premium office furniture. |
| First release | A small earned improvement and an invitation into the nearby community. The first outdoor visit is a shared neighborhood place. |
| Growing team | More useful workspace, books/storage, extra seating and personal touches. Preserve the small-town identity while the business grows. |
| Later success | Nicer furnishings and a larger, busier studio are earned upgrades. A phone booth is an optional later furnishing, not the starter's defining luxury. |

Use existing offline release/team progression rather than inventing a second
economy for this visual pass. Preserve items and progress already owned in
existing saves; adjust new-company grants and future unlocks explicitly.
Do not remove someone's furniture to make an older save match the new opening.

## Generation and admission

Every new furniture or environment brief names its intended progression stage.
For starter assets, state **cute, humble, handmade, restrained** and include
the established material board, the relevant source asset, and a same-scale
Godot calibration reference with characters and semantic surface landmarks.
The mixed-zoom material board alone cannot establish physical size.

Judge replacements in the actual occupied room/yard. Check their seat, desktop
and doorway relationships, visual detail, layer crossings and gameplay route.
An attractive standalone render can still be wrong for the opening. Follow
[ASSET_WORKFLOW.md](ASSET_WORKFLOW.md), [FURNITURE_SCALE_AUDIT.md](FURNITURE_SCALE_AUDIT.md)
and [MAP_WORKFLOW.md](MAP_WORKFLOW.md); preserve provenance and current evidence.
