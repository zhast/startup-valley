# Mina research four-way v2

Generated September 4, 2026 with built-in ImageGen by editing the retained v1
attempt. The requested row order was North, East, South, West, with four
standing research frames per direction, transparent background, no furniture,
and a fixed bottom-center anchor suitable for native 48 × 80 cells.

Disposition: **rejected for runtime; retained as visual reference only.** The
direction order and activity silhouettes are materially clearer than v1, but
the 1254 × 1254 PNG has no alpha channel and contains a painted checkerboard.
It therefore cannot be sliced or downscaled into the game without visible
background contamination and unstable pixel sampling.

Do not integrate this file. A subsequent attempt should request one directional
strip at a time on true transparency, or use a deterministic code-native
cleanup built from the reviewed native character sheets.
