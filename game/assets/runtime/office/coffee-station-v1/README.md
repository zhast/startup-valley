# Coffee station v1

Four independently authored code-native views of the shared coffee station.
South exposes the controls, group heads, drip tray, cup cubby and cabinet doors;
North shows the rear hatch, vents and cable; East and West show distinct
reservoir and vent ends. The rendered PNGs are not rotated or mirrored.

Every layer and whole reference uses a 192×256 canvas, floor pivot `(96,192)`,
native scale 1 and nearest filtering. The 40 runtime layers are byte-identical
copies of the reviewed `coffee-station-code-v1` fixture; the four whole PNGs are
retained as hash-locked reconstruction references.

This is an art/geometry promotion only. Coffee remains explicitly
**noninteractive**: the directional service sockets validate standing clearance
and approach geometry, but they do not enable clicking, pouring, drinking,
worker activity, persistence, or a new UI action.
